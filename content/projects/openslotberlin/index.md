OpenSlotBerlin is an online booking tool for appointment-based small businesses in Berlin. Customers select a service and an available time in the browser, without creating an account.

Businesses manage availability, breaks, and appointments in one place. Booking confirmations are sent by email and include a cancellation link. Booking data is stored in Europe.

[Visit OpenSlotBerlin](https://openslotberlin.de/)
